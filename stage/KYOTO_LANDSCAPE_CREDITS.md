# Kyoto vegetation and photographic ridges (2026-11-17)

Sakura and willow: JadeRealms / 3DModelHub free assets, https://3dmodelhub.io/ . Rendered in Blender into eight-direction atlases for use inside this game. Original models/textures are not shipped. Source receipts/license: kyoto-marathon/work/trees-landscape-2026-11-16/tree-assets/ . These are naturalistic authored models, not photographic scans. First Innerscene CC0 cherry candidate was rejected after visual comparison. Green tree: Tree Small 02 by Rico Cilliers, Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02 , CC0.

Mountain textures are adaptations of actual photographs, with sky and foreground masked and resized. Individual adapted textures retain their source license:

- kyoto-hiei*.png: Hahifuheho, Mt Hiei with Cherry Blossom, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mt_Hiei_with_Cherry_Blossom.JPG , CC0.
- kyoto-daimonji*.png: HmanJp, Nyoigatake long 001, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nyoigatake_long_001.jpg , CC0.
- kyoto-kitayama*.png: At by At, Kyoto Kitayama and Kamogawa River 2013-08, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kyoto_Kitayama_and_Kamogawa_River_2013-08.JPG , CC BY-SA 3.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ . Adapted images distributed under the same license.
- kyoto-nishiyama*.png: Pmx, Daimonji panorama, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Daimonji_panorama.jpg , CC BY-SA 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Adapted images distributed under the same license.

Original images, per-file license/API records and research comparison: kyoto-marathon/work/trees-landscape-2026-11-16/landscape-source/ . Position/height and masks are artistic approximations, not a surveyed reconstruction. No trees were modelled from scratch. Assets load with a fallback, and own their GPU resources. Full images are used on desktop; smaller atlases and mountain images are used for light quality. Buildings and ground retain their existing stage art.

Trees added 2026-11-18 (kyoto-pine-atlas*.png, kyoto-broad-atlas*.png): rendered in Blender into eight-direction atlases, no modelling.
- Pine: Pine Tree 01 (one of its three trees), Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_tree_01 , CC0. A European pine standing in for the Okazaki promenade pines; no free realistic Japanese black pine model was found.
- Second broadleaf: Island Tree 01, Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_01 , CC0.
- Street-tree gaps at side streets (src/track/KyotoStreetGaps.ts): © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
- Street-tree species by street: Kyoto City main street-tree map (FY2024). Research notes: kyoto-marathon/work/trees/tree-oss-research.md .
